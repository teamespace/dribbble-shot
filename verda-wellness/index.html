Your first eight weeks

Joining takes about a week. Here is exactly what happens, in order, so that none of it is a surprise.

/01 A twenty-minute call about what's actually going on. No forms yet.
/02 Full panel drawn, then a ninety-minute first visit with your clinician.
/03 Your plan arrives in writing: what changes, what we're watching, and why.
/04 Check-ins at six and twelve weeks, with markers repeated where it matters.
/05 A standing line to your care team for everything in between.

Questions we get
a lot.

What does membership include?–
Two full panels a year, two long visits, unlimited messaging with your care team, and same-week appointments when something comes up. Membership is $100 per year.
Do you take insurance?+
Membership itself is paid directly. Labs, imaging and prescriptions are billed to your insurer where they are covered, and we tell you which is which before anything is ordered.
Who is Verda for?+
Adults who want one clinician who knows their history, and who would rather sort something out early than manage it later. We are not an urgent care or an emergency service.
Where are you located?+
Our clinic is at 104 Wren Street, Austin, Texas 78703, with video visits available for anyone in Texas. Blood draws can be done here or at any of our partner labs.
Do I see the same clinician every time?+
Yes. You are matched to one clinician at the start and stay with them. If they are away, whoever covers has already read your plan.
How is my health data handled?+
Your records live in a HIPAA-compliant system, are never sold, and are shared only with clinicians you name. You can export or delete everything on request.
